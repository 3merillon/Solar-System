Quality

Presets set every control in the sections below. Changing any control switches the preset to Custom.

World and light
Terrain detail

Higher values keep fine detail farther away. 2x requests twice the range. The fixed terrain budget limits detail during rapid travel.

Scenery
Clouds and weather

Full resolution and 128 samples by default. Cloud travel and shape evolution animate independently; zero pauses that motion. The world Animate switch pauses all animation. More samples cost GPU time.

Ocean and underwater

Sea level changes the water datum; terrain stays in place.

Quality details

The resident cache allocation (memory tier) is chosen when the page loads; see Render diagnostics.
